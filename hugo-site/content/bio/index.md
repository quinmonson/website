Quin Monson is Professor of Political Science at Brigham Young University, a Senior Scholar with BYU's [Center for the Study of Elections and Democracy](http://csed.byu.edu/), and a Religion Fellow at BYU's [Wheatley Institute](https://wheatley.byu.edu/wheatley-fellows). He is the co-author of [*Seeking the Promised Land: Mormons and American Politics*]({{< staticrel "publication/seeking-promised-land/" >}}) (Cambridge University Press, 2014), and his research has appeared in a variety of academic journals including the *Journal for the Scientific Study of Religion*, the *American Political Science Review*, the *American Journal of Political Science*, the *Journal of Politics*, *Political Analysis*, and *Political Behavior*. He received his Ph.D. from Ohio State University in 2004.
